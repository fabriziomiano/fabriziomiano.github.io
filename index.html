About

A business intelligence leader shaped by research, delivery, and real business constraints.

I work at the intersection of technology, data, and business, leading Business Intelligence for the Americas Sales Organization at STMicroelectronics. My focus is on translating business priorities into scalable analytics and automation capabilities that support performance, planning, and decision-making.

My path spans data engineering, data science, business analytics, solution design, and project management across consulting, consumer goods, and the public sector. Before industry, experimental physics research at CERN shaped the analytical mindset I still bring to complex problems: make data not just accessible, but genuinely useful.
